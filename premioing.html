The Prize

In April 2008 the Representation in Italy of the European Commission told the school that "Live in Europe" was among the fifteen best websites in Italy. This is the letter.

EUROPEAN COMMISSION
REPRESENTATION IN ITALY
The Director
Rome, 17 April 2008
Liceo Scientifico "Galileo Galilei"
Bitonto (Bari)

Dear Giuseppe Marolla,

the selection committee of the "Europa alla lavagna" competition has completed its work, examining the website projects on the European Union prepared by upper secondary schools from all the Italian Regions.

The enthusiasm and commitment the students put into their projects are the clearest confirmation that the initiative launched by the Representation in Italy of the European Commission has aroused interest and has allowed students from all over Italy to take an active part in the debate on the future of the European Union.

As stated in the call, the selection committee chose the fifteen best projects on the basis of the completeness, relevance and consistency of their contents, the recognisability of the proposed themes, the originality of the site and its ability to explain and attract, its ease of navigation and the technical solutions adopted.

On the basis of the evaluation criteria set out in the call, your project has been selected among the fifteen best websites prepared by Italian schools.

Well done !!

As we announced when we launched this initiative, we invite you to celebrate Europe Day 2008 with the President of the Republic, Giorgio Napolitano, at a ceremony to be held at the Quirinale on 9 May, starting at 5 p.m.

In the coming days we will also send you more detailed information on the ceremony, as well as on your journey to and stay in Rome.

We can already tell you that your school's delegation will have to arrive in Rome on 8 May and leave on 10 May. Please also note that the number of participants may under no circumstances exceed the number of people indicated on the participation form, which we kindly ask you to return to us completed by 22 April.

Please inform the students and teachers of your school that on that occasion the President of the Republic will deliver a speech addressed to all Italian schools.

Furthermore, if you intend to organise events or initiatives on Europe later this school year or during the next one, we are ready to help you with our advice and information.

Thank you for having contributed, through your work, to spreading the idea of Europe.

Pier Virgilio Dastoli
