However, despite all this interest, many questions, which regard, particularly our nation, remain sill opened. We may think to migratory flows, from various years, large boats full of immigrants, who see in our country the only way to escape difficult situations, continue to land an our coasts. An initial, but maybe utopian, intervention by EU, to break this tragically interventions, might be a decisive and legitimate assistance to these growing nations, which are not able to prove themselves in autonomy way. I don’t know neither how much has been done more how much has been thinking to do, but I’m sure that it is possible to solve this grievous problem, because the supranational union of the states was born in order to sustain the growing of the inners states, also starting from all what geographically surrounds these ones. Following, there is the problem of criminality, which, starting from a small scale, inside the small communities, emerges in international criminality, which encumber with entire union. An intervention form the smaller sphere, the daily and scholastic sphere, surely would be very important. School have to be seen has places of education about how, about becoming hones citizens and in this all the EU, assuring to local institutions founds in order to start projects and activities, is contributing a lot in improving ethic and social growing of us. I think, however, that contemporary society shakes in being bearer of values as it was before, everything is a but lost and forgotten: the twenty-first century’s events, the continuous state of war, and, why not, medias are the principal cause of violence, of diffused and almost implacable unlawfulness. But them, giving up for a while politic, which young don’t believe a lot in, how is possible, in a substantial way, to live in laws full way, feeling ourselves in a full way citizen of the world? I thing that the best thing to do is to stop a while a think at our future, at our dreams, at our hopes, at our realizations, at all what will come with us and after us; thinking at that is not possible that we don’t want to put on an apron and started working: future is our thing!
